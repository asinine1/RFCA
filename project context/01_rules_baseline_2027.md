The current Colorado CSEF student page says that students must be in grades

This means DMRSEF and CSEF are not two independent entry opportunities. They
are stages in one affiliated-fair pathway.

## 2. Conservative project baseline

For the initial project, we should deliberately keep the study computational
and non-clinical:

| Area | Working boundary | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Research subject | Hybrid discrete-diffusion and autoregressive language-model architecture and generation | Keeps the scientific object software/method performance, not a human condition |
| Data | Synthetic sequences first; then only public/pre-existing data with verified license, provenance, and version | Avoids recruitment and protects against private-data mistakes |
| Human interaction | None: no surveys, interviews, recruitment, user testing, or collecting data from classmates | Avoids starting a human-participant study accidentally |
| Personal data | No private chats, accounts, names, contact data, or identifiable records | Public availability alone is not permission to ignore privacy or license terms |
| Biology/health | No biological samples, clinical records, medical claims, diagnosis, treatment, or health application | Avoids human-participant, tissue, biological-agent, and medical-risk pathways |
| Hardware/site | Home, school, or ordinary personal computing for the initial phase | Work at a regulated research institution or industrial setting may add Form 2C and adult-support documentation |
| Product testing | No public app or participant evaluation in the initial study | Testing a student-designed application with people other than the permitted sole tester can require IRB review |

Under the current ISEF human-participant rules, publicly available or published
pre-existing data with no human interaction can fall under an exempt data/record
review category. That is a conditional classification, not an automatic waiver:
the dataset's access conditions, de-identification, license, and exact use still
have to be checked. A dataset containing identifiable private information, or
any new interaction or collection, changes the analysis.

The safest first dataset is therefore synthetic data generated by the project,
followed by a small, explicitly licensed or public-domain text corpus whose
source, license, download date, and cryptographic hash are recorded.

## 4. Forms and approval gates

The current 2027 ISEF and Colorado CSEF materials indicate the following
starting set for every high-school project:

- Form 1, Checklist for Adult Sponsor / Safety Assessment
- Form 1A, Student Checklist / Research Plan
- Research Plan / Project Addendum, as applicable
- Form 1B, Approval Form
- Form 2A, Student Support Disclosure Form; CSEF identifies this as new for
  2027 and required for all projects

The Research Plan must describe the rationale, research question or hypothesis,
expected outcomes, materials, procedures, risk and safety, data analysis, and
bibliography. It must be prepared before experimentation. If a mentor or other
adult contributes, the plan must distinguish the student's work from that
support.

For the conservative computational baseline, these forms are **not currently
expected** solely because we train a model:

- Form 4 or an IRB, provided there is genuinely no human-participant study
- Forms 5A/5B, because there are no vertebrate animals
- Forms 6A/6B, because there are no potentially hazardous biological agents,
  tissue, or body fluids
- Form 3, unless the actual activities, devices, or site introduce a hazard

The following are conditional and must be re-evaluated before use:

- **Form 2B:** a Qualified Scientist is involved or required
- **Form 2C:** work occurs at a regulated research institution, industrial
  setting, or comparable external research site
- **Form 3:** the actual hardware, activity, chemical, or site creates a hazard
- **Form 4 and IRB review:** any recruitment, interaction, survey, testing by
  other people, identifiable private information, or new human data collection

If the project becomes a human-participant study, IRB approval must occur before
recruitment or interaction. Consent/assent and parental permission may be
required, and Form 4 plus the appropriate consent documents must be maintained.
After initial approval, changes to the Research Plan or procedures require the
relevant re-approval before work resumes.

Even for the low-risk computational path, the project should not be treated as
paperwork-free: all-project forms, the Research Plan, adult review, safety
assessment, final SRC review, and the fair's submission process still matter.

## 5. Eligibility and project ownership checks

For the ISEF-eligible Senior Division path, current ISEF rules require grades
9–12 (or equivalent) and age under 20 on May 1 preceding ISEF. DMRSEF and CSEF
also serve middle-school students, but that does not by itself establish ISEF
eligibility.

Across the pathway:

- A student may enter only one ISEF-affiliated project and compete through only
  one affiliated-fair route, subject to the affiliated progression to a state
  or national fair.
- A team may contain no more than three students. DMRSEF expects substantial,
  explainable contribution from every team member; CSEF keeps team projects
  within the same division.
- The student project must represent the student's own portion of the work.
  Adult, mentor, paid, institutional, and AI support must be disclosed.
- A project based on earlier work may be a continuation only when the new work
  is a substantive expansion or new line of investigation. Repeating the same
  question and method with more data is not enough; Form 7 may be required.
- The current ISEF rules limit the judged work to a continuous period of no
  more than 12 months within the applicable project window. The start date,
  end date, and any continuation relationship must be recorded from the first
  qualifying experiment.

The current project facts supplied by the student are: **individual project**,
**sophomore**, and intended Senior-Division DMRSEF path. The exact school,
county, and age still need to be checked against the registration record.
Having had an idea earlier does not by itself make this a continuation project;
the continuation question turns on whether qualifying prior research or
experimentation in a similar area was performed. At present we will treat this
as a new project, with continuation status to be rechecked against any prior
work log.

## 6. Generative-AI and authorship boundary

The 2027 ISEF rules permit AI as a project resource but require proper
acknowledgment and independent student work. The Society's AI-use table says,
among other things:

- idea development can be acceptable with a prompt log when applicable;
- initial project code can be acceptable only with explicit disclosure of which
  portions were AI-generated and a prompt log;
- AI may help identify statistical tests or tools, but interpretation of the
  data must be done by the student researcher and the prompts logged;
- generative AI may not initially write the Research Plan, abstract, paper, or
  poster, may not write conclusions or future steps, and may not create a
  starter bibliography or citations.

This folder is an internal planning notebook, not a submission artifact. Any
future Research Plan, abstract, poster, conclusions, bibliography, or final
interpretation must be written and verified by the student researcher under
the current fair policy. We should maintain a separate AI-use log from the
start.

research location, outside mentorship, or the research question—we stop and
re-run the classification rather than quietly continuing under the old plan.

## 8. Risks to keep visible

- **Local-rule drift:** DMRSEF's 2027 forms are explicitly pending. Recheck the
  official page before registration and again before submission.
- **False exemption:** converting, anonymizing, or downloading data does not
  automatically make a source exempt; provenance and privacy conditions still
  matter.
- **External compute/support:** a university cluster, lab, or substantial
  mentor contribution may change forms and the description of student work. A
  rented commercial GPU is not automatically a regulated research site, but
  its provider, access arrangement, adult support, and actual research
  location should still be disclosed and checked. Work physically conducted
  in a university or industrial research setting is a separate risk/form
  question.
- **AI authorship:** generated code and planning assistance need a log and
  disclosure; generated submission prose or invented references are not valid.
- **Continuation status:** if this develops from a previous memory or language
  model project, the new question and variables must be demonstrably different.
- **Scientific validity:** a lower loss at a higher compute cost is not an
  efficiency result. Every claim must report matched controls and both quality
  and cost.
